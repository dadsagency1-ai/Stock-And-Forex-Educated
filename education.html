04 / Risk

Risk & Awareness

Trading and investing involve risk. Educational material cannot remove market risk or guarantee results.

Common mistakes

  • Using money that cannot comfortably be lost.
  • Oversizing a position or trading without a defined risk limit.
  • Following a signal or social-media claim without independent research.
  • Making decisions based on emotion after a loss or win.

Transparency note

  • No guaranteed returns or “sure-shot” profit claims are made on this website.
  • Content is general education and information, not individualized financial advice.
  • Visitors should verify applicable rules and seek qualified professional advice where appropriate.
